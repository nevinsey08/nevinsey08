## Featured Research

**From Cash to Cashless: Evaluating PayNow and Cash Demand in Singapore**

An econometric research project conducted under the NUS–MOE Humanities and Social Sciences Research Programme (Economics), under the supervision of Professor Huang Ta-Cheng at the National University of Singapore. The study examines whether PayNow's introduction in 2017 reduced aggregate cash demand in Singapore. Using R, I apply SARIMA interrupted time-series modelling to Singapore's currency-in-circulation-to-GDP ratio, construct a counterfactual without PayNow, and conduct placebo and falsification tests to assess the robustness of the findings.
